# Corporate Travel Policy

## TP-1.1 Advance Booking

Domestic travel must be booked at least 7 days before departure. International travel must be booked at least 14 days before departure. Shorter notice is an exception and requires manager approval.

## TP-2.1 Travel Class

Economy class is mandatory by default. Premium Economy or Business is permitted only if the flight duration is 6 hours or more AND the employee grade is L5 or above. Otherwise the booking must be changed to Economy.

## TP-2.2 Fare Caps (round trip)

Economy domestic cap is INR 15,000. Economy international cap is INR 90,000. Business class international cap is INR 120,000. Fares above the cap must be replaced by a compliant fare under the cap.

## TP-4.1 Department Budget

The selected fare must not exceed the remaining travel budget of the employee's department. If no compliant fare fits the remaining budget, the request requires manager approval.

## TP-5.1 Preferred Carriers

Contracted airlines (AirIndia, IndiGo, Vistara-AI) are preferred. A non-contracted airline may be chosen only if it is cheaper than the best contracted option by at least 10% or no contracted option exists.

## TP-6.1 Business Purpose

Travel must be for a business purpose (client meeting, conference, training, project delivery, audit). Personal, leisure or unspecified purposes are not eligible and require manager review.

## TP-7.1 Trip Frequency

An employee may take at most 3 trips per calendar quarter. A 4th or later trip in the same quarter requires manager approval.

## TP-9.1 High Value Approval

Any single request whose selected fare exceeds INR 100,000 requires manager approval.
