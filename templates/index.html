Deterministic Clause Precedence Hierarchy

When multiple rules trigger on a request, primary clause assignment obeys strict precedence:

1. TP-6.1 (Purpose) 2. TP-1.1 (Advance Notice) 3. TP-7.1 (Trip Frequency) 4. TP-9.1 (High Value >₹100k) 5. TP-4.1 (Dept Budget) 6. TP-2.1 (Travel Class) 7. TP-2.2 (Fare Caps)
Corporate Travel Policy Knowledge Base (RAG Chunks)
TP-1.1 Advance Booking

Domestic travel must be booked ≥7 days before departure. International travel requires ≥14 days. Shorter notice escalates to manager approval.

TP-2.1 Travel Class

Economy is mandatory by default. Business/Premium Economy permitted only if flight ≥6 hours AND grade ≥L5. Otherwise modified to Economy.

TP-2.2 Fare Caps (Round-Trip)

Economy domestic cap: INR 15,000. Economy international cap: INR 90,000. Business international cap: INR 120,000.

TP-4.1 Department Budget

Selected fare cannot exceed remaining department budget. If no compliant fare fits budget, manager approval is required.

TP-5.1 Preferred Carriers

Contracted airlines (AirIndia, IndiGo, Vistara-AI) are preferred. Non-contracted allowed only if ≥10% cheaper than best contracted option.

TP-6.1 Business Purpose

Travel must be for business purposes (client meeting, conference, training, project delivery, audit). Personal/leisure trips require manager review.

TP-7.1 Trip Frequency

Max 3 approved trips per calendar quarter. A 4th or later trip in the same quarter escalates to manager approval.

TP-9.1 High Value Approval

Any single trip exceeding INR 100,000 requires manager approval (outranks TP-4.1 and TP-2.2).
