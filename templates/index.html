Deterministic Clause Precedence Hierarchy

When a request triggers multiple policy clauses, the primary decision and scoring clause is resolved deterministically by strict precedence order:

1. TP-6.1 (Business Purpose) 2. TP-1.1 (Advance Notice) 3. TP-7.1 (Trip Frequency) 4. TP-9.1 (High Value >₹100k) 5. TP-4.1 (Department Budget) 6. TP-2.1 (Travel Class) 7. TP-2.2 (Fare Caps)
Travel Policy Clauses (data/policy/travel_policy.md)
TP-1.1 Advance Booking

Domestic travel must be booked at least 7 days before departure. International travel must be booked at least 14 days before departure. Shorter notice requires manager approval.

TP-2.1 Travel Class

Economy class is mandatory by default. Premium Economy or Business is permitted only if flight duration is ≥ 6 hours AND employee grade is L5 or above. Otherwise booking must be changed to Economy.

TP-2.2 Fare Caps (Round Trip)

Economy domestic cap: INR 15,000. Economy international cap: INR 90,000. Business class international cap: INR 120,000. Fares above cap must be replaced by compliant fare.

TP-4.1 Department Budget

Selected fare must not exceed remaining travel budget of employee's department. If no compliant fare fits remaining budget, request requires manager approval.

TP-5.1 Preferred Carriers

Contracted airlines (AirIndia, IndiGo, Vistara-AI) are preferred. Non-contracted airlines allowed only if cheaper by at least 10% or no contracted option exists.

TP-6.1 Business Purpose

Travel must be for a valid business purpose (client meeting, conference, training, project delivery, audit). Personal or leisure purposes require manager review.

TP-7.1 Trip Frequency

An employee may take at most 3 trips per calendar quarter. A 4th or later trip in the same calendar quarter requires manager approval.

TP-9.1 High Value Approval

Any single travel request whose selected fare exceeds INR 100,000 requires manager approval (takes precedence over TP-4.1 and TP-2.2).
